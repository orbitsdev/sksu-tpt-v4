TPT admin flow

What the admin does in one TPT season, in order, from setting up the year to the interview. Written for review before we finalize. Settings below are as the live site stands on Nov 2, 2026.

The season at a glance

  1. 1Set up the year
  2. 2Add and publish a schedule
  3. 3Applications open
  4. 4Applications close
  5. 5Exam day
  6. 6Import test scores
  7. 7Release results
  8. 8Interview and closing

The Dashboard follows this path: its numbers change with the stage, and Needs attention lists what to do now. Each card on Exam schedules always says the next step.

1

Set up the year

Once a year, before anything is public

  1. School years: name, testing fee and how examinee numbers look. From the second year on, Set up next school year copies last year so you change only what changed.
  2. Campuses and programs: tick what each campus offers, then set each cut-off and whether it counts the Overall or ESM score.
  3. Rooms: add each exam room and its seats, per campus.
  4. Staff and roles: tap Add staff, type the name and email and pick a job. They sign in with Google at that email.
  5. Results › Signatories: names, positions and signatures for the result slip. Set once a year.
  6. Make current when the year is ready.
Nothing shows to students yet. They see nothing until the year is current and a schedule is published.
2

Add and publish a schedule

Exam schedules, for each TPT round

  1. Tap Add schedule. Set when applications open and close, the Closing time (usually 11:59 PM), the last day to pay, and the report and start times.
  2. The next page is Set up exam slots. Pick the exam days once. Each campus already shows its building, rooms and seats; change what differs and untick days a campus isn't using. One button makes every slot.
  3. Tap Publish on the schedule's card. A date already past is refused.
Not publishedOnly staff can see it.
Opens Mar 1Published, waiting for its date.
Applications openStudents can apply.
Applications closedPayment and slots may continue.
Exam dayCheck-in is running.
Exam doneNext: import, then release.

The status moves by date. Nobody has to press anything for these changes.

3

Applications open

The admin's daily work

A student applies, uploads the receipt and sends it. Then an admin or a Receipt checker checks it. A super admin gives that job in Staff and roles, to one person or several, and can limit it to a campus:

  1. Open Receipts to check, oldest first. Compare the amount and reference number with the GCash or bank record.
  2. Approve it, or send it back with a reason the student will read.
  3. Open Support tickets and answer what's waiting. Tick close when it's solved.
ApprovedThe student gets an examinee number and picks an exam slot. They then get a room, a seat and a permit.
Sent backThe student sees "Needs a fix" with your reason, fixes it and resends.

When one student needs something

Find them with search or Applications, open their page, then choose:

  • Send message to write to this one student.
  • Edit their details, with a reason that stays in the history.
  • Move their exam slot, or book one for a paid applicant with no phone.
  • Under …: more days to pay, move to a later schedule, change sign-in email or cancel.

When many students need to know

Announcements: start from a ready message, pick who gets it, see how many that is, then send in the app only or by email too.

When seats run low

The bell warns at 9 of 10 seats taken and again when full. Tap Add more seats on the slot or add another exam day. Paid students with no seat are told when new seats open.

4

Applications close

By date, or sooner by hand

By itself: applications stop at the closing date and time. Those still unpaid after the last day to pay are cancelled that night at 12:10 AM. Each student is told and must apply again.
  • Close applications now stops new applicants at once. Those who already applied can still pay and pick a slot.
  • Reopen applications lets them apply again, up to the limit the system allows.
  • Edit can move the last day to pay. Moving it earlier warns first, with how many unpaid applications will close.
  • Postpone moves the exam, for example because of a storm. Seated students are told and permits update by themselves.
  • Change times on a slot moves its times. Seated students are told.
5

Exam day

Proctors at the rooms, admin on the Dashboard

  1. Print the Seat plan from the slot if the room needs one.
  2. Proctors open Check-in, pick a room and tap Here for each student who arrives.
  3. Anyone can scan a permit's QR code to see Valid permit with the name, photo and exam details.
  4. The Dashboard shows who's here, who isn't yet and which rooms need a look.

A student with no phone is found by last name in Check-in, then checked against an ID.

6

Import test scores

After the exam, when the Testing Center sends the Excel file

  1. Open Import test scores, pick the schedule and upload the file.
  2. Wait for the check. You can leave the page; the bell says when it's done.
  3. Fix any rows with problems. Each row says what to do, and they can be downloaded as Excel.
  4. Check the summary, including how many have no score, then tap Approve.
By itself on approval: students with a seat, a passed exam day and no score become Absent. Result PDFs start preparing in the background.
7

Release results

Students see nothing until this step

  1. Open Release results and watch the PDFs finish.
  2. Tap Publish results and confirm. Or choose Prepare, then publish and it publishes on the release date once every PDF is ready.
  3. Students are told in waves: in the app, by phone alert and by email.
Qualified for a programAnswers the survey, sees the result and chooses a program. The app says the next step is an interview and what to bring.
Not qualifiedSees "Thank you for taking the test", advice about other institutions and a Support link.
AbsentThe admin can move a student with a valid reason to a later schedule. They keep their payment and number.

Survey before the result is on, so the scores, PDF and program choice open after the survey. To fix one result, Hide it or Correct the score with a reason. Its PDF is made again and the change is logged.

8

Interview and closing

The office takes over from the app

  1. Announce interview dates on the official page, and send Announcements with the Interview schedule template to students who chose a program.
  2. Use Reports and Statistics for the office: applicants, collections, attendance, scores, ranking and survey results.
  3. Start the next schedule at step 2, or close the school year from School years.
Not in the app: interviews aren't scheduled or tracked here. The office sets them, and the app only tells students to expect one.

Decisions before we finalize

Does the Dec 13 transferee test run in the system or on paper?

Recommended: paper, because the poster already tells students to download the form. Test the transferee flow for the next round.

May students who didn't qualify take the test again?

The result screen doesn't offer another schedule today. If retakes are allowed, it needs to say so.

Do interview dates differ by program or campus?

Announcements can't pick students by program yet. If dates differ, that filter should be added.

Should there be a button to end the last day to pay early?

Recommended: no. One wrong tap could close every unpaid application. Edit already does it, with a warning.
