# "For" line on exam schedules (semester and academic year)

Oct 31, 2026. Decision (owner): **add a "For" line**, keep one TPT season.

## Why

The transferee TPT on Dec 13, 2026 is "for TRANSFEREES for the SECOND SEMESTER of the Academic Year 2026–2027",
while the site's current school year (TPT season) is 2027–2028, the freshmen intake for next June. The app has no
semester; everything hangs on the season (fee, examinee numbers, programs, cut-offs). Showing "SY 2027–2028" to
transferees joining in January 2027 is wrong.

## Change

1. **Schedules get an optional "For" text** (up to 80 characters), e.g. `2nd semester, AY 2026–2027` or
   `1st semester, AY 2027–2028`. Empty = the season's name, as today.
2. **Shown where students see their exam:** the home page key dates and schedule cards, student Home, the review step,
   the permit (screen and PDF) and the result slip (PDF). Admin: the Exam schedules list and form.
3. **Suggested text:** a new transferee schedule suggests "2nd semester, AY {season − 1}"; freshmen suggest
   "1st semester, AY {season}". The office can change it.
4. **Overlap rule:** two schedules of the **same type** can't take applications on the same days. A transferee
   schedule may run while the freshmen schedule is open.

## Kept the same

One TPT season at a time: same fee, examinee numbering, programs and cut-offs for its schedules.
A separate school year per semester stays out of scope (bigger change; decide with the office if ever needed).

## Setting up the Dec 13 transferee exam (after the change)

- Transferees switched on (`TPT_TRANSFEREES=true`).
- Schedule: type Transferees, For "2nd semester, AY 2026–2027", applications Nov 1 – Dec 6, pay by Dec 9.
- Room at ACCESS: building "Guidance and Testing Center", room "Testing Room, 2nd Floor".
- Exam slots Dec 13 at ACCESS: 1st batch report 7:30 AM, starts 8:00 AM; 2nd batch report 12:30 PM, starts 1:00 PM.
