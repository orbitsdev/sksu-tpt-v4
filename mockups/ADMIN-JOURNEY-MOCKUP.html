Admin: the full season

Mockup for review · 28 Oct 2026

Everything the admission office does in one season, in order: sign in, set up the season, build the exam schedule, run registration, approve payments and issue numbers, run exam day, import scores, publish results, then report. Every form and every state is its own frame. Numbers are screens; orange numbers are states of the screen before them.

Calm rules

One green button per areaDanger actions are red and always behind a confirm.
Every change has a reasonEdits, moves and corrections ask why and write the history.
Nothing waits on a spinnerExports, emails and score maths run in the background.
Tables fold on a phoneEach row becomes a card; filters stack.
Guards, not warningsCan’t remove the last admin, can’t double-approve, can’t overbook.
Same words as the applicant seesDraft, In review, Approved, Scheduled, Scored.
