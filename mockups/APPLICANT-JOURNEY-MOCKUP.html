Calm rules

Big and few wordsLexend, 16 px inputs, 60 px buttons, one action per screen.
Never lose workAutosave, offline typing, retry on upload.
Say what happens nextEvery success says the next step; every error says how to fix it.
Safe answer firstDialogs put Cancel or Back below the main action.
Status in wordsColour never carries meaning alone.
Phone firstEvery applicant screen is drawn at 390 px.
