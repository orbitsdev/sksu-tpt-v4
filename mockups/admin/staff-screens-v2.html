Staff screens, new design

What staff see when they sign in. Each person sees only their own job, so the screen stays short. All names and numbers are samples. Each screen is shown as a laptop and as a phone.

Who gets what
  • Receipt checker: checks payment receipts and looks up results at the office.
  • Support desk: answers student questions.
  • Proctor: marks who came to the exam room.
  • Cashier and Program chair stay off for now. Their screens come when those switches are turned on.
