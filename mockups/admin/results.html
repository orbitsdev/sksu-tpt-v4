Guide · Results

What this page is for

Shows the approved scores of a schedule and controls when students see them.

How it works

  1. Set a release date and time, or press Publish now.
  2. Students get a notification and an email when results are published.
  3. Hide results takes them back down; emails already sent stay sent.

Examples

  • Release Apr 20, 9:00 AM.
  • Qualified for 1st choice: overall score at or above the program's cut-off.
