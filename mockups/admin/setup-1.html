Guide · New school year

What this page is for

Sets up a whole school year in five steps. Everything is saved as a draft; students see nothing until you publish Schedule 1.

How it works

  1. Basics: school year, exam fee, first examinee number, payment methods.
  2. Schedules: up to three or more, with dates.
  3. Campuses and programs: copied from last year; switch off what isn't offered.
  4. Exam slots and rooms.
  5. Review and publish.

Examples

  • SY 2028–2029 · fee ₱275.00 · first examinee no. 600001.
  • Copying last year saves typing campuses, programs, rooms and staff.
