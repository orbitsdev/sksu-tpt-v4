What each part answers, and why this chart

PartThe questionShown as
Season lineWhere are we in the year, how long is left?Timeline. A chart would add nothing here.
Needs you todayWhat must I do now?Short list that opens the right page. It sits first because the office acts on it.
4 numbersHow are we doing?Number tiles, with a thin meter where there is a total.
Where applicants areWhere is everyone, and where do they get stuck?Stepped bars on one scale, with the gap to the next step. This is the overview, and it stays all year.
Applications per dayIs it speeding up? Do we need more staff?Area line, because it is a change over time.
Seats by exam dayWhich day or campus fills up first?Table with meters, so exact counts stay readable.
By campusWhich campus has the most?Sorted bars. No pie chart: long lists of slices are hard to compare, mostly on a phone.
Stanine spreadHow did this batch score?Columns 1 to 9, because the order of the scale matters.
Office workAre receipts and support keeping up?Plain counts, plus the oldest item waiting.
