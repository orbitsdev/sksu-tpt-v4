Guide · Staff and roles

What this page is for

Who can sign in to the admin, cashier and proctor screens, and what each role can do.

How it works

  1. Staff join by invite only: enter their email and role.
  2. Change a role at any time; it applies on their next click.
  3. The Roles tab shows exactly what each role can do.

Examples

  • Cashier 1 · Isulan counter 1.
  • Program chair · BS Information Technology, or College dean · every program in the college: approves transferees.
