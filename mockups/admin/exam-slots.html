Guide · Exam slots

What this page is for

An exam slot is a campus and a date. Students pick one; their room and seat are assigned from the real room sizes.

How it works

  1. Add an exam slot: campus, date, rooms.
  2. The seats add up from the rooms you pick.
  3. When a slot is full it closes by itself; you can also close it.
  4. Assign rooms and seats places everyone who hasn't been placed yet.

Examples

  • Isulan · Sat, Apr 25 · 4 rooms · 200 seats.
  • Report by 7:30 AM, exam starts 8:00 AM: one guide time for the day; there are no morning or afternoon groups.
