Reports and statistics

Statistics and Reports are two separate pages. Statistics shows the numbers and charts at a glance. Reports works like the Settings page: a search box and the grouped list of reports on the left, the chosen report on the right. Each report has the same filters (school year, exam, campus) and the same Export menu: PDF, HTML page, or Excel.

Do we have reports today?
Yes, 12 reports and 4 charts on Admin → Reports. They are spread over one long page, and only some can be exported (Excel).
What changes
One organized Reports page, a Statistics overview that compares with the last exam, new Top students and Top programs reports, and PDF / HTML / Excel on every report.
Who sees it
Admins. Deans and chairs could later see their own college only (to decide).
