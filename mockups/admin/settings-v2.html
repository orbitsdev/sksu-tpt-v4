Settings, receipt approvers and support assignment

Start like the old TPT, then switch extras on from one organized Settings page (the Euphoria way). Many staff can approve receipts and answer tickets, not only one admin account. Click the switches, tabs and search to try them; this page saves nothing. Use Desktop / Phone and Light / Dark / Auto (top right) to see every screen at phone size and in both themes.

Do we have a page to approve applicants?
Yes: Receipts to check. Today only Admins can open it. This mockup adds a Receipt checker role so many staff can approve.
Who approved in the old TPT?
One shared admin account. In v4 every staff member signs in with their own Google account, so each approval shows who did it.
Concerns and tickets?
A Support desk role. New tickets are given out to support staff automatically, or an admin assigns them.

What this adds
  • Where admins see results today: Admin → Scores and results (release, the list, and each student's result page with Share). That stays for admins.
  • New: a simple Result lookup for receipt checkers, so many staff can help at the office on release day.
  • Search by name, examinee no. or code, or scan the permit QR.
  • All campuses: the lookup is never limited to the staff member's campus. A student who took the test in Kalamansig can ask at ACCESS. The campus filter starts at "All campuses".
  • Is this the student? Staff ask the birth date and type it (the system says if it matches, without showing it), then compare the ID and face with the 2×2 photo. The result stays hidden until both are done, so it is not given to a classmate or someone else asking.
  • "Did not take the exam" (absent): paid and had a slot, but was not checked in on exam day, so there is no score. Opening it shows the slot they missed and what they can do next (the next schedule, if the office allows a retake).
  • Read-only: staff cannot change scores or results.
  • Survey first still holds online. At the office the slip can be printed anyway, and the student is asked to answer the survey in the app.
  • Every print and view is logged with the staff name.
