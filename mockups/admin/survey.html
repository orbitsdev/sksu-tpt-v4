Guide · Survey

What this page is for

The survey students answer after their result and before choosing a program. The default statements are the ones the office already submits.

How it works

  1. Edit, add, reorder or switch off statements for a school year.
  2. Once students have answered, saving starts a new version; old answers keep the statement they answered.
  3. Results and the Excel for the office are under Survey results.

Examples

  • 1. The pre-registration process is easy and convenient.
  • Answers: Strongly Agree to Strongly Disagree.
