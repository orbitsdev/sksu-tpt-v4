Guide · Exam schedules

What this page is for

A school year has one or more exam schedules. Each schedule has its own registration dates, exam days, exam slots and result release. Nothing is public until you publish it.

How it works

  1. Add a schedule with its registration dates, exam days and result date.
  2. Add exam slots for it (campus and date) on the Exam slots page.
  3. Press Publish. Students can apply only while it is published and registration is open.
  4. Unpublish stops new applications; people who already applied keep their place.

Examples

  • Schedule 1 · registration Jan 1 – Feb 28 · exam Mar 14–15 · results Apr 20.
  • Schedule 4 is for transferees: the program chairperson approves before they pay at the cashier.
  • Absent in Schedule 1? With the switch on, they can take Schedule 2 without paying again.
