Counter v2 · plan and mockup

A calmer counter for rush days: one search, one applicant at a time, and three clear steps (Check → Pay → Done), with the camera, receipt checks and the OR booklet built in. Fictional names only.

Plan

Flow in three steps new

  • Check: big photo, name, birth date, school, programs. "Looks right" or "Take photo".
  • Pay: amount, received, change, OR, Cash / GCash.
  • Done: examinee no., print slip, book a slot, next applicant.

One search, three lists better

  • Search matches as you type: name, code, examinee or OR no.
  • Tabs: Waiting to pay (applied date/time, pay-by), Receipts to check, Paid today.
  • Arrow keys move, Enter opens, Esc goes back.

OR booklet in the header fix

  • Shows the booklet in use, the next OR and how many are left.
  • An OR outside the booklet asks for a reason (or adding a booklet).
  • A used OR is refused.

Camera at the counter new

  • "Take photo" when the 2×2 is missing, blurry or not them.
  • Webcam or phone camera; the photo replaces the old one on the permit.

Receipts to check better

  • Receipt photo large (zoom), reference no., day paid, amount.
  • Approve gives the examinee no.; Send back with reasons (tick boxes).

Every payment is traceable already

  • Saved with school year, schedule (via the application), counter, campus, cashier, shift, time, method and OR.
  • "My shift" = what this cashier took since Start shift (for the cash count).
