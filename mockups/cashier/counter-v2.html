Payment desk v2 · plan and mockup

A calmer counter for rush days: one search, one applicant at a time, and three clear steps (Check → Pay → Done), with the camera and receipt checks built in. Fictional names only.

"Counter" is only a name for a desk. Whoever takes the fee there (dean's office staff, admission staff, Cashier's Office or any SKSU employee) just needs the Takes payments job on their staff account. Each desk has its own shift and cash count.

Plan

Flow in three steps new

  • Check: big photo, name, birth date, school, programs. "Looks right" or "Take photo".
  • Pay: amount, received, change, OR, Cash / GCash.
  • Done: examinee no., print slip, book a slot, next applicant.

One search, three lists better

  • Search matches as you type: name, code, examinee or OR no.
  • Tabs: Waiting to pay (applied date/time, pay-by), Receipts to check, Paid today.
  • Arrow keys move, Enter opens, Esc goes back.

OR number checks already

  • The OR is typed from the paper receipt (7 digits).
  • An OR already used anywhere in SKSU this school year is refused, with who used it and when.
  • A torn or misprinted OR can be marked spoiled.

Camera at the counter new

  • "Take photo" when the 2×2 is missing, blurry or not them.
  • Webcam or phone camera; the photo replaces the old one on the permit.

Receipts to check better

  • Receipt photo large (zoom), reference no., day paid, amount.
  • Approve gives the examinee no.; Send back with reasons (tick boxes).

Every payment is traceable already

  • Saved with school year, schedule (via the application), counter, campus, cashier, shift, time, method and OR.
  • "My shift" = what this staff member took since Start shift (for the cash count).

Shift left open new

  • At 6 PM a staff member with a shift still open gets a reminder to end it.
  • Next morning they count yesterday's cash first.
  • Admins see every shift still open and can close it for them, with a note.

Refunds new

  • Paid twice, or the exam was cancelled: the office records the refund in the app (why, how, voucher no., amount).
  • Fees collected shows collected, refunded and net.
  • The student sees "Refunded ₱275 on Nov 3".

Receipt warning signs new

  • The app looks for signs of an edited or reused receipt and shows them on the receipt.
  • They are hints, not proof: staff still look, then approve or send back.

No scrolling through a list. The student shows a QR code on their phone; the staff member scans it and lands on step 1 with that student open.

Three ways to find them

  • Scan the QR with the laptop webcam, a phone camera or a USB QR scanner (it types the code by itself). About 1 second.
  • Type the last digits of the code, like 54452. When only one student matches, they open at once.
  • Type the name, surname first or not: villanueva jose. Then pick from a short list with the photos.

No phone? The code is also on the email and the printed application. The list below stays for walk-ins and checking who is waiting.

After "Next applicant" the search box is ready again, so the next scan works right away.

What the app checks

  • Same photo as another student's receipt already
  • Same reference no. as another receipt already
  • Looks like another receipt: the same picture with small changes, like a new number or name typed over it new
  • OR no. already used at an SKSU counter for someone else new
  • Dates don't fit: paid after the day it was sent, or before applications opened new
  • Saved by an editing app, when the photo still says so new

Most receipts are honest, so a sign only asks staff to look closely. A screenshot alone is not a sign, since many students pay by GCash.
