Guide · Cashier counter

What this page is for

Records exam fee payments at the counter. Recording a payment approves the application and gives the student an examinee number.

How it works

  1. Find the person: type their name, application code (APP-27-…) or pick them from the waiting list.
  2. Check the name and program on the right.
  3. Type the official receipt number from your booklet and how they paid.
  4. Press Enter. The student sees their examinee number in the app right away.

Examples

  • Lea M. Sampulna · APP-27-10422 · ₱275.00 cash · OR 0048214.
  • OR already used? The screen says who it was used for; type the next number.
  • Paid already? The screen shows when and where; don't take a second payment.
