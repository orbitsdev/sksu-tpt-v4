Privacy notice

Who we are

Sultan Kudarat State University, through its Guidance and Testing Center, EJC Montilla, Tacurong City, runs the SKSU Tertiary Placement Test (SKSU TPT). Data Protection Officer: [DPO NAME], [DPO EMAIL].

What we collect

About you
name, date of birth, sex, home address, mobile number, email
School
school, year graduated, strand, general average; transferees: current school and course, and a grades file
Your choices
three programs (two for transferees) and your exam slot
Photo
your 2×2 ID photo, printed on your permit
Payment
the official receipt (OR) number and where you paid
Exam
examinee number, scores and stanines from the Testing Center
Afterwards
your survey answers and support messages

Why

To register you, schedule your exam, issue your permit, release your result and process your admission. We rely on your consent and on the university's function of admitting students.

Who can see it

Admission staff
your whole application, to run the test
Cashiers
your name, application code and payment only
Program chairpersons and deans
transferee applicants to their own program or college only
Proctors
the list for their room, only if check-in is used
The Testing Center
examinee numbers, to score the test

Companies that host the website and send our emails and texts handle your data only to deliver this service. We never sell your data and share it with no one else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Applications and results are kept for [RETENTION PERIOD, to confirm with the DPO], then deleted or made anonymous. Photos are deleted [PHOTO RETENTION] after the school year ends.

How we protect it

The site uses an encrypted connection. Staff see only what their role needs, and every staff action is logged.

Your rights

  • Be told how your data is used (this notice)
  • See a copy of your data
  • Correct wrong data
  • Object to how it is used
  • Ask us to delete it when we no longer need it
  • Get your data in a common file format
  • Complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph)

To use them, open Support in the app and pick the topic Privacy request, email sksutpt@gmail.com, or email the Data Protection Officer.

Cookies

We use only what is needed to keep you signed in. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Terms of use

  • One account per person. Do not apply for someone else.
  • Your information must be true. By applying you certify that all information you give is true and correct, and that any misrepresentation may cause disqualification from admission to SKSU.
  • Keep your permit to yourself. It is checked against your photo and ID on exam day.
  • Use Support for real questions. Repeated or abusive messages may pause your support for 7 days.
  • The admission office may cancel an application that has false information.
