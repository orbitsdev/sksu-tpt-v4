Install SKSU TPT on your phone

Free, about 1 MB, no Play Store or App Store. It opens like an app and keeps your permit even without signal.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open sksu-tpt.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app when it appears, or tap then Install app.
  3. Tap Install. The SKSU TPT icon is now on your home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open sksu-tpt.com in Safari.
  2. Tap Share at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen , then Add.

No space or an old phone? You can also use SKSU TPT in the browser. Everything works the same.
